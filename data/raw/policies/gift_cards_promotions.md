# ShopSmart Electronics — Gift Cards, Store Credit & Promotions Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2024
**Policy Version:** 2.2

---

## 1. Gift Cards

### 1.1 Purchasing Gift Cards

ShopSmart gift cards are available in digital and physical formats:

| Format | Denominations | Delivery | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital (email) | $10 – $500, any whole amount | Within 15 minutes | None |
| Digital (scheduled) | $10 – $500 | On the chosen date | None |
| Physical card (mailed) | $25, $50, $100, $200 | 5–7 business days | None |
| Physical card (in-store) | $10 – $500 | Immediate | None |
| Corporate bulk order | $25 – $1,000 per card, 25 card minimum | 7–10 business days | None |

Gift cards are **non-returnable and non-refundable** once purchased. This applies regardless of account tier and regardless of how soon after purchase the request is made. A gift card purchase cannot be cancelled after the digital card has been delivered to the recipient.

### 1.2 Using Gift Cards

Gift cards never expire and carry no inactivity, dormancy or maintenance fees. Balances may be applied to any purchase except the purchase of another gift card. A gift card may be combined with exactly one other payment method per order; the gift card balance is always applied first.

Gift cards cannot be used to pay for:

- Other gift cards or prepaid cards
- ShopSmart Premium membership fees
- ShopSmart Financing Card balances
- Outstanding chargeback or dispute amounts

### 1.3 Lost or Stolen Gift Cards

Digital gift cards can be reissued if the original purchaser provides the order ID and the card has an unspent balance. Physical gift cards are treated like cash and **cannot be replaced** unless the customer registered the card to their account before it was lost. Registration is free and available at help.shopsmartelectronics.com/giftcards.

### 1.4 Refunds Paid to Gift Cards

When an item purchased with a gift card is returned, the refund is issued as **store credit**, never as cash or a card refund. This is a strict rule with no exceptions, including for Premium Members and including for defective items. Customers who need cash back should use a different payment method at the time of purchase.

## 2. Store Credit

### 2.1 How Store Credit Is Issued

Store credit is created in these situations:

1. A refund where the customer elected store credit instead of the original payment method
2. A refund where the original payment method is expired, closed or otherwise unusable
3. A refund of a gift card purchase
4. A price adjustment where the customer chose instant store credit over a card refund
5. Goodwill credits issued by support at supervisor discretion
6. The Premium Member annual birthday credit of $10.00

### 2.2 Store Credit Terms

| Attribute | Rule |
|---|---|
| Expiry | Never expires, except promotional and goodwill credits |
| Promotional credit expiry | 60 days from issue |
| Birthday credit expiry | 60 days from issue |
| Transferable | No — tied to the issuing account |
| Redeemable for cash | No |
| Usable toward membership fees | Yes |
| Usable toward gift card purchases | No |
| Refund processing time | Instant |
| Combinable with other payment | Yes, applied first |

Store credit is the fastest refund route available. Where a customer is frustrated by card refund timelines of 5–10 business days, offering instant store credit is the appropriate first resolution.

### 2.3 Store Credit and Returns

If an item purchased using store credit is returned, the store credit portion is restored to the account immediately upon inspection approval. Any portion paid by card is refunded to that card on the normal timeline. This mixed restoration is itemized on the refund confirmation email.

## 3. Promotional Codes

### 3.1 Code Types

| Code Type | Typical Value | Stacking | Applies To |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome code (new accounts) | 10% off, max $50 | Not stackable | First order only |
| Seasonal sale code | 5–20% off | Not stackable with other codes | Eligible categories |
| Category code | 10–15% off | Not stackable | Named category only |
| Free shipping code | Shipping waived | Stackable with one discount code | Any order |
| Referral credit | $20 store credit | Stackable | Any order over $100 |
| Clearance markdown | Varies | Not stackable | Marked items only |

Only **one percentage-discount code** may be applied per order. A free shipping code may be combined with one percentage-discount code. Store credit and gift card balances are not promotional codes and may always be applied.

### 3.2 Code Exclusions

Promotional codes do not apply to:

- Gift cards and prepaid cards
- ShopSmart Protect extended warranty plans
- Premium membership fees
- Installation, delivery and White Glove service fees
- Items already discounted below the manufacturer's minimum advertised price
- Pre-orders, unless the code explicitly states pre-order eligibility
- Orders already receiving a price match

### 3.3 Codes and Returns

When an order with a promotional discount is partially returned, the discount is recalculated across the retained items. If removing the returned item drops the order below the code's minimum purchase threshold, the discount is forfeited and the refund is reduced accordingly.

**Worked example.** An order of two items totalling $220.00 used a "$30 off orders over $200" code, so the customer paid $190.00. Returning the $120.00 item leaves a $100.00 order, below the $200.00 threshold. The discount is forfeited: the customer is refunded $190.00 − $100.00 = $90.00, not the $120.00 list price of the returned item. This recalculation is shown on the refund confirmation.

If the full order is returned, the entire amount paid is refunded and the promotional code is restored to the customer's account where the code is single-use and still within its validity period.

## 4. Loyalty Points

### 4.1 Earning

All registered accounts earn ShopSmart Points on completed purchases:

| Account Tier | Points per $1 spent | Bonus Categories |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 1 point | — |
| Premium | 2 points | 3 points on Audio & Headphones |
| Premium Student | 2 points | 3 points on Laptops & Computers |
| Premium Business | 2 points | 3 points on Computer Accessories |

Points post to the account **14 days after delivery**, aligned with the shortest standard return window, so that points are not awarded on items that are promptly returned.

### 4.2 Redeeming

100 points equal $1.00 in redemption value. Points may be redeemed in increments of 500 points ($5.00) and applied to any purchase except gift cards and membership fees. Redeemed points are deducted at checkout and appear as a discount line on the invoice.

### 4.3 Points and Returns

When an item is returned, the points earned on that item are reversed. If the points have already been redeemed and the account balance is insufficient to absorb the reversal, the account carries a negative point balance that is offset by future earnings. ShopSmart does not charge the customer cash for a negative point balance.

Points expire after **24 months** of account inactivity, defined as no purchase and no redemption. A single qualifying purchase resets the inactivity clock on the entire balance.

## 5. Bundle and Multi-Item Offers

### 5.1 Bundle Pricing

Bundles combine a primary product with accessories at a combined price below the sum of individual prices. Bundles are sold as a single SKU with a single order line.

### 5.2 Returning Part of a Bundle

A bundle must be returned in full to receive a refund. Individual components of a bundle cannot be returned separately, because the bundle price is not allocated to individual items. If a customer wishes to keep part of a bundle, the options are:

1. Return the complete bundle and reorder the desired item at its individual price
2. Keep the complete bundle

The sole exception is a defective component: a defective item within a bundle is replaced individually at no charge, with the rest of the bundle retained by the customer.

### 5.3 Bundle Return Windows

The bundle's return window is governed by the **shortest** window among its components. A bundle containing a smartphone (15-day window) and headphones (30-day window) carries a 15-day window for the entire bundle. Premium Member extensions apply to the resulting bundle window.
