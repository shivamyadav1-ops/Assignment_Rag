### 1.2 Authorization vs. Capture

When an order is placed, ShopSmart places an **authorization hold** on the payment method for the order total. Funds are not captured until the order ships. For orders shipping in multiple packages, each shipment captures only the portion of the total covering the items in that package.

Authorization holds typically release within 3–5 business days if an order is cancelled. The exact release timing is controlled by the issuing bank and is outside ShopSmart's control. For debit cards, hold release may take up to 10 business days.

### 1.3 Split Payment

A single order may combine at most **two** payment methods: one store-credit or gift-card balance plus one card, PayPal or BNPL method. Store credit and gift card balances are always applied first, with the remainder charged to the secondary method.

## 2. Refund Processing

### 2.1 Refund Timeline Breakdown

A refund passes through three stages. The total time a customer experiences is the sum of all three:

| Stage | Duration | Controlled by |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Warehouse receipt and inspection | 1–2 business days | ShopSmart |
| 2. Refund initiation and release | Same day as inspection approval | ShopSmart |
| 3. Funds appearing in the customer's account | Varies by method (see 1.1) | Issuing bank / payment provider |

Total end-to-end time from return shipment to funds received is typically **7–14 business days**. Store credit refunds bypass stage 3 entirely and are available instantly after inspection approval.

### 2.2 Refund Destination Rules

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Exceptions:

1. **Gift card purchases** are refunded as store credit, never to a card or bank account.
2. **Expired or closed cards** — if the original card is no longer valid, the refund is issued as store credit. The customer may contact support to request a bank transfer, which requires identity verification and takes 10–15 business days.
3. **Split payments** are refunded proportionally to each method, with the store-credit portion returned first.
4. **BNPL orders** — the refund is sent to the BNPL provider, which then adjusts the customer's payment plan. Remaining scheduled installments are reduced or cancelled. The customer continues to owe any installments already due at the time of refund until the provider processes the adjustment.
5. **Cash in-store purchases** over $250.00 are refunded by corporate check mailed within 10 business days, not in cash.

### 2.3 Partial Refunds and Deductions

The refund amount may be reduced by:

- **Return shipping label fee:** $7.95 for standard accounts on non-defective returns; waived for Premium Members and for all defective returns
- **Large-item pickup fee:** $29.95 standard; $14.95 for Premium Members on items over 50 lbs; waived for defective items
- **Restocking fee:** 15% on opened televisions over 55 inches, desktop computers, large appliances and drones
- **Late-return reduction:** returns accepted after the standard window but within 60 days of delivery are refunded at 85% of the price paid
- **Missing accessories:** replacement cost of each missing item is deducted
- **Condition deduction:** items showing use beyond normal inspection may be refunded at 70% at manager discretion

Deductions are itemized on the refund confirmation email. Deductions never apply to items confirmed as defective, Dead-on-Arrival, or shipped in error.

Promotional financing is deferred-interest, not waived-interest. If the balance is not paid in full by the end of the promotional period, interest is charged retroactively from the original purchase date at the standard APR.

### 3.2 Buy Now Pay Later

BNPL splits the order total into 4 interest-free payments taken every two weeks, with the first payment due at shipment. Eligibility is determined by the BNPL provider via a soft credit check that does not affect credit score. Orders between $50.00 and $4,000.00 are eligible.

Returns on BNPL orders follow the standard Return & Refund Policy. The refund is routed through the BNPL provider; customers should expect 7–14 business days for the payment plan to be adjusted.

## 4. Billing Issues

### 4.1 Duplicate Charges

If a customer sees two charges for one order, the second is almost always a pending authorization that has not yet released. Verification steps:

1. Confirm only one order exists in Order History
2. Check whether one charge is marked "pending" by the bank
3. If both charges are posted and settled after 5 business days, contact support with both transaction IDs
4. Confirmed duplicates are refunded within 2 business days

### 4.2 Failed Payments

An order with a declined payment is held in "Payment Pending" status for **48 hours**. ShopSmart retries the payment method twice during that period and emails the customer after the first failure. If payment is not resolved within 48 hours, the order is automatically cancelled and inventory released.

### 4.3 Chargebacks and Disputes

Customers are asked to contact ShopSmart support before filing a bank dispute, as most issues resolve faster directly. When a chargeback is filed:

- The disputed order is frozen and any pending return or refund is suspended
- ShopSmart responds to the issuing bank within 7 business days with order and delivery evidence
- Bank resolution typically takes 45–90 days
- Accounts with repeated invalid chargebacks may be suspended and barred from future financing

A chargeback filed while a return is in progress will delay the refund until the dispute is resolved, because the funds cannot be released twice.

## 5. Taxes, Fees and Invoices

### 5.1 Sales Tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address and applicable state and local rates. Tax is refunded proportionally on returned items. Tax-exempt organizations must register an exemption certificate with the Business Accounts team before placing orders; exemptions cannot be applied retroactively to completed orders.

### 5.2 Invoices and Receipts

An order confirmation email is sent immediately after purchase. A tax invoice is generated at shipment and available under Order History → Invoice. Business accounts may request consolidated monthly invoicing. Invoices remain downloadable for 7 years from the purchase date.
