# ShopSmart Electronics — In-Store & Additional Services Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2024

## 1. Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store (BOPIS)

### 1.1 How It Works

Select "Store Pickup" at checkout and choose a ShopSmart retail location. In-stock items are ready within **2 hours** during store hours. The customer receives an email and SMS notification when the order is ready for collection.

| Attribute | Rule |
|---|---|
| Readiness time (in stock) | 2 hours |
| Readiness time (ship-to-store) | 3–5 business days |
| Hold period | 7 days from the ready notification |
| Fee | None |
| Payment | Authorized online, captured at pickup |
| ID required | Yes — government photo ID matching the order name |

Orders not collected within 7 days are cancelled automatically and the authorization hold released. No cancellation fee applies.

### 1.2 Authorized Pickup by Another Person

An alternate pickup person may be designated during checkout or by editing the order before it is marked ready. The alternate must present their own government photo ID and the order ID. Without a designated alternate on the order, store staff may not release the merchandise to anyone other than the account holder, regardless of what information the person presents. This restriction exists to prevent fraudulent collection and cannot be waived by store staff.

### 1.3 Returns of BOPIS Orders

BOPIS orders may be returned in store or by mail. The return window is measured from the **pickup date**, not the order date. In-store returns of BOPIS orders are inspected immediately and refunds initiated the same day, which is faster than the 1–2 business day warehouse inspection for mailed returns.

## 2. In-Store Returns

### 2.1 What Can Be Returned In Store

Any item purchased online or in store may be returned to any ShopSmart retail location within the applicable return window, with these exceptions:

1. Items over 50 lbs or with a longest dimension over 48 inches must use scheduled pickup, not in-store return
2. Items requiring hazardous-material handling, including loose lithium batteries
3. Items purchased through the Business Accounts team under a negotiated contract
4. International orders, which must be shipped to the domestic return center

### 2.2 What to Bring

- The item in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals and documentation
- Proof of purchase: order confirmation email, printed receipt, or the order ID
- The payment card used, where a card refund is requested
- Government photo ID

Returns without proof of purchase are denied. Store staff cannot look up an order by name alone. A customer who cannot locate their order ID should contact support before visiting the store.

### 2.3 In-Store Refund Timing

| Original Payment | In-Store Refund Method | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Credit card | Same card | 5–7 business days to post |
| Debit card | Same card | 5–10 business days to post |
| PayPal | PayPal account | 3–5 business days |
| Store credit / gift card | Store credit | Instant |
| Cash under $250 | Cash | Immediate |
| Cash $250 and over | Corporate check by mail | 10 business days |

Store staff cannot issue cash refunds for card purchases under any circumstances.

## 3. Trade-In Program

### 3.1 Eligible Categories

ShopSmart accepts trade-ins of smartphones, tablets, laptops, smartwatches, gaming consoles and cameras. Trade-in value is quoted online or in store based on model, age and condition.

| Condition Grade | Description | Typical Value |
|---|---|---|
| Excellent | No scratches, fully functional, all original accessories | 100% of quote |
| Good | Light cosmetic wear, fully functional | 75–90% of quote |
| Fair | Visible wear, fully functional, may be missing accessories | 40–60% of quote |
| Poor / Recycle | Non-functional, cracked screen, water damage | $0 – recycling only |

### 3.2 Trade-In Process

1. Obtain an online quote or in-store appraisal
2. Erase all personal data and disable activation locks — devices with an active lock are returned to the customer and earn no credit
3. Submit the device in store, or ship it with the prepaid label provided within 14 days of the quote
4. ShopSmart inspects and confirms the grade within 3 business days of receipt
5. Credit is issued as store credit, instantly on confirmation

### 3.3 Quote Revisions and Disputes

If inspection finds the device's condition lower than the customer's self-assessment, ShopSmart issues a revised offer by email. The customer has **7 days** to accept the revised offer or request the device be returned at no charge. No response within 7 days constitutes acceptance of the revised offer.

Trade-in credit is issued as store credit only. It cannot be paid in cash, applied to a card, or transferred to another account. Trade-in credit does not expire.

Premium Members receive 15% off all installation services. Service availability depends on the delivery postcode; availability is confirmed at checkout.

### 4.2 Scheduling and Cancellation

Appointments are scheduled in 4-hour windows. Cancellation or rescheduling is free with at least **24 hours** notice. Cancellation with less than 24 hours notice, or a missed appointment where the technician cannot gain access, incurs a $49.00 trip fee.

### 4.3 Service Guarantee

Installation work carries a 90-day workmanship guarantee. If the installation fails due to workmanship, ShopSmart returns at no charge. The guarantee covers the installation only, not the product itself, which remains under its own warranty terms.

### 4.4 Services and Refunds

Installation and service fees are refundable only if the service has not yet been performed. Once a technician has completed the appointment, the fee is non-refundable even if the associated product is subsequently returned. A product return does not automatically cancel a scheduled installation — the customer must cancel the appointment separately.

## 5. Repairs

### 5.1 In-Warranty Repairs

Products under the ShopSmart Standard Warranty, an active ShopSmart Protect plan, or a manufacturer warranty may be submitted for repair at any retail location or by mail using a prepaid label. Turnaround is typically 7–14 business days from receipt, extending to 14–21 days if parts must be ordered.

### 5.2 Out-of-Warranty Repairs

Repairs outside any warranty are quoted before work begins. A non-refundable diagnostic fee of $49.00 applies and is credited toward the repair if the customer proceeds. If the quoted repair exceeds 70% of the product's current replacement cost, ShopSmart recommends replacement instead and applies the diagnostic fee as store credit toward a new purchase.

### 5.3 Data Responsibility

Customers are responsible for backing up their data before submitting any device for repair. ShopSmart is not liable for data loss during repair, and repair processes frequently require a full device wipe. Staff should confirm verbally that the customer has backed up their data and record that confirmation on the repair ticket.

### 5.4 Loaner Devices

Loaner devices are available only to ShopSmart Protect **Plus** customers, dispatched within 2 business days of claim approval. Loaners are not available under Protect Basic, the Standard Warranty, or for out-of-warranty repairs. The loaner must be returned within 7 days of the repaired device being delivered; late returns are charged $15.00 per day up to the loaner's replacement value.

## 6. Recycling and Disposal

ShopSmart accepts electronic waste for free recycling at all retail locations regardless of where the item was purchased, with a limit of 3 items per customer per day. Accepted items include computers, phones, tablets, televisions under 50 inches, small appliances, cables and batteries. Not accepted: items containing refrigerants, smoke detectors, and any device with visible battery swelling or leakage, which require specialist hazardous-waste handling.

Recycling does not generate store credit. Customers seeking value for a working device should use the Trade-In Program instead.
