# ShopSmart Electronics — Return & Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2024  
**Policy Version:** 3.2

---

## 1. General Return Policy

ShopSmart Electronics accepts returns on most items within a specified return window from the date of delivery. All returned items must be in their original packaging, include all accessories, manuals, and documentation, and show no signs of physical damage beyond normal inspection.

Proof of purchase (order confirmation email, receipt, or order ID) is required for all returns. Returns without proof of purchase will be denied.

### 1.1 Standard Return Windows

| Product Category | Return Window | Condition Required |
|---|---|---|
| Smartphones & Tablets | 15 days | Unopened or defective only |
| Laptops & Computers | 30 days | Original packaging, all accessories |
| Audio & Headphones | 30 days | Original packaging required |
| Televisions & Monitors | 15 days | Unopened or defective; in-box return only |
| Cameras & Photography | 30 days | Original packaging, all accessories |
| Gaming Consoles | 15 days | Unopened or defective only |
| Gaming Accessories | 30 days | Original packaging required |
| Smart Home Devices | 30 days | Original packaging required |
| Wearables & Fitness | 30 days | Original packaging required |
| Computer Accessories | 45 days | Resalable condition |
| Cables & Adapters | 45 days | Unopened only |
| Software & Digital Codes | Non-returnable | — |
| Gift Cards | Non-returnable | — |

### 1.2 Premium Member Extended Returns

Customers enrolled in the ShopSmart Premium Membership program receive an additional 15-day extension on all standard return windows. For example, a laptop purchased by a Premium member may be returned within 45 days instead of the standard 30 days.

Premium membership status is verified at the time the return is initiated, not at the time of original purchase.

### 1.3 Holiday Return Policy

Items purchased between November 15 and December 31 of any calendar year may be returned until January 31 of the following year, or within the standard return window — whichever is later. This applies to all returnable product categories.

## 2. Refund Methods

### 2.1 Original Payment Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase. Processing times vary by payment type:

| Payment Method | Refund Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Credit Card | 5–7 business days |
| Debit Card | 5–10 business days |
| PayPal | 3–5 business days |
| Store Credit | Instant |
| Gift Card | Refunded as store credit |
| Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) | 7–14 business days |

### 2.2 Store Credit Option

Customers may choose to receive their refund as store credit regardless of the original payment method. Store credit refunds are processed instantly and do not expire. Store credit is non-transferable and cannot be redeemed for cash.

### 2.3 Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be issued in the following situations:

- Item returned after the standard return window but within 60 days of delivery: **85% refund**
- Item returned with missing accessories or packaging: **refund minus replacement cost of missing items**
- Item showing signs of use beyond normal inspection: **70% refund at manager discretion**

### 2.4 Restocking Fees

A restocking fee of **15%** applies to the following categories when the item has been opened:

- Televisions over 55 inches
- Desktop computers and workstations
- Large appliances
- Drones and aerial equipment

No restocking fee is charged for defective items or items shipped in error.

---

## 3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned under any circumstances:

1. Software with activated license keys or redeemed digital codes
2. Gift cards and prepaid cards
3. Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance — No Returns" at the time of purchase
4. Customized or personalized products
5. Items with tampered or missing serial number labels
6. Hazardous materials including batteries sold separately
7. Intimate or personal hygiene electronics (e.g., electric shavers, personal care items) once opened

---

## 4. Defective Items & Warranty Returns

### 4.1 Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) Policy

Items that are non-functional upon delivery are classified as Dead-on-Arrival. DOA claims must be filed within **48 hours** of delivery. DOA items receive:

- Full refund OR immediate replacement
- Free return shipping
- Priority processing (1–2 business days)

### 4.2 Manufacturer Warranty Claims

For items beyond the ShopSmart return window but within the manufacturer's warranty period, customers should contact the manufacturer directly. ShopSmart can assist with providing proof of purchase documentation.

### 4.3 Extended Warranty (ShopSmart Protect)

Customers who purchased ShopSmart Protect extended warranty coverage receive:

- Extended coverage for 1 or 2 additional years beyond manufacturer warranty
- Accidental damage protection (drops, spills, cracked screens)
- No deductible for the first claim; $50 deductible for subsequent claims
- Free shipping for warranty repairs

## 5. Return Process

### 5.1 Initiating a Return

Returns can be initiated through any of the following channels:

1. **Online:** Log into your ShopSmart account → Order History → Select item → "Return Item"
2. **Phone:** Call customer support at 1-800-SHOPSMART (1-800-746-7762)
3. **In-Store:** Bring the item and proof of purchase to any ShopSmart retail location
4. **Chat:** Contact our live chat support through the ShopSmart app or website

### 5.2 Return Shipping

- **Defective items:** Free return shipping via prepaid label
- **Non-defective returns:** Customer pays return shipping, or a flat $7.95 is deducted from the refund for a prepaid label
- **Large items (TVs over 40", desktops, monitors over 32"):** Scheduled pickup required; $29.95 fee deducted from refund for non-defective returns; free for defective items

### 5.3 Return Processing Timeline

Once the returned item is received at our warehouse:

1. **Inspection:** 1–2 business days
2. **Refund initiation:** Same day as inspection approval
3. **Refund received:** Varies by payment method (see Section 2.1)

Total estimated time from return shipment to refund: **7–14 business days**

---

## 6. Exchanges

ShopSmart offers direct exchanges for the same item in a different color, size, or configuration. Exchanges follow the same return window as returns. If the replacement item costs more, the customer pays the difference. If it costs less, the difference is refunded.

Exchanges are subject to product availability. If the desired replacement is out of stock, the customer may choose to wait for restocking or receive a full refund.

---

## 7. International Orders

Returns for international orders must be shipped to our domestic return center. The customer is responsible for:

- Return shipping costs
- Any customs duties or import taxes on the return shipment

International returns are processed within 10–15 business days of receipt. Refunds are issued in the original currency of purchase.

---

## 8. Fraud Prevention

ShopSmart reserves the right to limit or refuse returns from customers who abuse the return policy. Indicators of return abuse include:

- Excessive return rate (more than 40% of orders returned over a 12-month period)
- Patterns of returning used items as unopened
- Serial number mismatches
- Returns of counterfeit or substituted items

Accounts flagged for return abuse may be suspended pending review.
