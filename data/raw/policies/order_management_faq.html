Cancelling an Order

How long do I have to cancel an order?

Orders can be cancelled without charge for 1 hour after placement, provided the order has not yet moved to "Processing" status. Most orders remain in "Confirmed" status for at least one hour, but high-demand items and Next-Day Shipping orders may enter processing in as little as 15 minutes.

Once an order reaches "Processing," it cannot be cancelled. The customer must accept delivery and then use the standard return process described in the Return & Refund Policy.

What happens to my money when I cancel?

Because funds are only captured at shipment, a cancelled order releases its authorization hold rather than issuing a refund. The hold typically clears in 3–5 business days for credit cards and up to 10 business days for debit cards. Store credit and gift card balances are restored immediately.

Can I cancel part of an order?

Partial cancellation is available only while the entire order remains in "Confirmed" status. Select the specific items in Order History and choose "Cancel Selected Items." Once any item in the order enters processing, partial cancellation is disabled and the remaining items must be returned after delivery.

My order was cancelled without my request. Why?

ShopSmart may cancel an order automatically in these situations:

  • Payment failure: payment could not be collected within 48 hours of the first decline
  • Inventory shortfall: the item sold out between order placement and picking
  • Pricing error: the item was listed at an obviously incorrect price
  • Address verification failure: the shipping address could not be validated and the customer did not respond within 72 hours
  • Fraud screening: the order was flagged by automated risk checks
  • Quantity limit: the order exceeded the per-customer purchase limit on a restricted item

In all automatic-cancellation cases the authorization hold is released and the customer receives an email explaining the reason. No cancellation fee is ever charged.

Modifying an Order

Can I change the shipping address after ordering?

Address changes are possible only while the order is in "Confirmed" status. After the order enters processing, the address is locked. For orders already shipped, customers may attempt to redirect the package through the carrier's website using the tracking number, though carrier redirection is not guaranteed and may add 1–2 days to delivery.

Can I change the shipping speed after ordering?

Upgrading shipping speed is possible within the 1-hour "Confirmed" window by contacting support; the price difference is charged to the original payment method. Downgrading shipping speed is not available because the original shipping charge has already been authorized; the customer may cancel and reorder instead.

Can I add items to an existing order?

Items cannot be added to an existing order. Place a separate order for the additional items. If both orders are placed within 2 hours and ship to the same address, ShopSmart will attempt to consolidate them into a single shipment automatically, though this is not guaranteed.

Can I change the payment method after ordering?

Payment method changes require cancelling the original order and placing a new one. Support cannot swap a payment method on an existing authorization. The exception is a failed payment, where the customer is invited to supply a new method within the 48-hour payment-pending window.

Backorders and Pre-Orders

What is a backorder?

A backorder is placed when an item is temporarily out of stock but expected to be restocked. The product page displays an estimated availability date. Backordered items are not charged until they ship. Customers may cancel a backordered item at any time before it ships, without charge.

If a backordered item's availability date moves back by more than 30 days, ShopSmart notifies the customer and offers the choice of continuing to wait, substituting an equivalent product, or cancelling with no charge.

How do pre-orders work?

Pre-orders are placed for products not yet released. Key rules:

  • Payment is authorized at order placement and captured when the item ships, not at pre-order time
  • Pre-orders may be cancelled at any time before the item ships, without charge
  • If the manufacturer's price drops between pre-order and release, the customer is charged the lower price automatically — this is the Pre-Order Price Guarantee
  • If a release is delayed beyond 60 days from the announced date, ShopSmart cancels the pre-order and releases the hold; the customer may re-order at the new date
  • The return window for pre-ordered items begins on the delivery date, not the pre-order date

What if only part of my order is in stock?

ShopSmart ships in-stock items immediately and sends the remaining items as a separate shipment at no extra shipping cost. This is called a split shipment. Each shipment has its own tracking number, and each item's return window begins on the date that specific item was delivered — not the date of the first shipment.

Split Shipments and Multi-Package Orders

Why did my order arrive in multiple boxes?

Orders split into multiple packages when items ship from different fulfillment centers, when an item is oversized and requires separate freight handling, or when one item is backordered. Split shipments never incur additional shipping charges to the customer.

How are refunds handled on split orders?

Each item is refunded individually based on its own delivery date and category return window. Shipping charges, where applicable, are refunded only when every item in the order is returned. Returning one item from a multi-item order does not refund the order's shipping charge.

One box arrived but the other is missing. What should I do?

Check whether the missing items show a separate tracking number in Order History — they may still be in transit with a later delivery date. If the second tracking number shows "Delivered" but nothing arrived, follow the lost-package process: wait 24 hours, then contact support. Claims must be filed within 7 days of the delivery date shown in tracking.

Order Troubleshooting

My order status hasn't changed in several days.

Orders normally move from "Confirmed" to "Processing" within 1–2 business days, and to "Shipped" within a further 1–2 days. During peak periods (mid-November through December) processing can take up to 3 business days. If an order has remained in the same status for more than 4 business days outside peak season, contact support and reference the order ID.

I never received an order confirmation email.

Confirmation emails are sent within 5 minutes of a successful order. If none arrives:

  1. Check spam and promotions folders
  2. Verify the email address on the account is correct
  3. Check Order History — if the order appears there, it was placed successfully and the email simply failed to deliver
  4. If the order does not appear in Order History and no charge or hold appears on the payment method, the order was not completed

Can I place an order on behalf of someone else?

Yes. Enter the recipient's name and address as the shipping address while keeping your own billing details. Note that the account holder, not the recipient, must initiate any return or warranty claim. Returns can only be processed by the account that placed the order, which is why gift recipients should ask the purchaser to start the return.

Can I look up an order without logging in?

Guest order lookup is available using the order ID together with the email address used at checkout. For security, guest lookup shows status and tracking only. Returns, cancellations and refunds require account access or a verified support call, because those actions require identity verification against the account that placed the order.
