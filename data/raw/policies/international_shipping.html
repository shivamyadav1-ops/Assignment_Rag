2. Customs Duties, Taxes and Fees

Who pays customs charges?

The recipient is responsible for all customs duties, import taxes, VAT/GST and carrier brokerage fees. These are assessed by the destination country's customs authority based on the declared value and product classification. ShopSmart does not control, collect or receive any portion of these charges and cannot estimate them reliably in advance.

What happens if the recipient refuses to pay duties?

A package refused at customs is returned to ShopSmart or abandoned by the carrier, depending on the destination. If the package is returned to ShopSmart:

  • The item price is refunded in the original currency of purchase
  • The original international shipping charge is not refunded
  • Any return shipping cost billed by the carrier is deducted from the refund
  • Processing begins only on receipt at the domestic return centre, typically 15–30 days after refusal

If the package is abandoned or destroyed by customs, no refund is issued, because the goods were delivered to the carrier as contracted and lost through non-payment of duties.

Can the declared value be reduced?

No. ShopSmart declares the actual transaction value on all customs documentation. Requests to under-declare value, mark an order as a gift, or omit items from the declaration are declined. Falsifying customs documentation is illegal in both the origin and destination countries.

3. Product Restrictions

Items that cannot ship internationally

  1. Lithium batteries over 100Wh shipped by air — this affects large power stations, some drones and certain laptop spares. Devices with the battery installed are generally permitted; loose spare batteries are not.
  2. Region-locked products — gaming consoles, some software and certain media devices carry licensing restrictions by territory.
  3. Oversized items exceeding 70 lbs or 108 inches in combined length and girth.
  4. Items containing refrigerants or pressurized gases.
  5. Products subject to export control, including certain high-performance computing and encryption hardware, to specific destinations.

The checkout system blocks ineligible items for the selected destination before payment. If an item becomes restricted after an order is placed, the order is cancelled and the authorization hold released, with no cancellation fee.

Warranty on international orders

The ShopSmart 90-day Standard Warranty applies internationally. Manufacturer warranties, however, are frequently territory-limited: a manufacturer may decline service on a unit purchased in another region. Customers should verify the manufacturer's international service policy before purchase. ShopSmart Protect plans are available only for domestic delivery addresses and cannot be purchased for international orders.

4. Cross-Border Returns

How do international returns work?

International returns must be shipped to the domestic return centre. ShopSmart does not provide prepaid international return labels for non-defective returns. The process:

  1. Initiate the return through Order History within the applicable return window
  2. Receive the Return Merchandise Authorization and the return centre address
  3. Ship the item using a tracked service at the customer's expense
  4. Mark the shipment clearly as "Returned Goods" to avoid import duties being charged to ShopSmart on re-entry
  5. Processing begins on receipt, typically 10–15 business days

Import duties already paid to the destination country are not refundable by ShopSmart under any circumstances. Many countries operate a duty drawback scheme allowing the importer to reclaim duties on re-exported goods; customers should contact their national customs authority.

Do return windows differ internationally?

No. The standard category return windows apply identically to international orders, measured from the delivery date. Premium Member extensions also apply. Because international transit adds time, customers are advised to inspect and test items promptly on arrival.

In what currency is the refund issued?

Refunds are issued in the original currency of purchase to the original payment method. Where the customer's bank converted the original charge, the refunded amount in local currency may differ from the amount originally debited because exchange rates move between the two transactions. ShopSmart does not compensate for exchange rate movement or for foreign transaction fees charged by the customer's bank.
