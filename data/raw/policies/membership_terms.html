1. Program Overview

ShopSmart Premium is a paid annual membership program that provides enhanced shipping, extended return privileges, exclusive pricing and priority customer support. Membership is tied to a single ShopSmart account and may not be shared across multiple accounts or households.

2. Membership Benefits

2.1 Shipping Benefits

Premium Members receive free Expedited Shipping (2–3 business days) on all domestic orders with no minimum purchase requirement. Standard (non-member) accounts receive free Standard Shipping (5–7 business days) only on orders of $35.00 or more.

Premium Members may upgrade to Next-Day Shipping for a discounted fee of $9.99 instead of the standard $24.99. Premium Same-Day delivery in eligible metro areas is discounted to $19.99 from $34.99.

Premium Business members additionally receive free Next-Day Shipping on orders over $500.00.

2.2 Extended Return Window

Premium Members receive an additional 15 days added to every standard return window. A product category with a standard 30-day return window becomes 45 days for a Premium Member; a 15-day window becomes 30 days; a 45-day window becomes 60 days.

The extension applies to the return window only. It does not change restocking fees, non-returnable item categories, or the condition requirements stated in the Return & Refund Policy. Items classified as non-returnable remain non-returnable regardless of membership tier.

Membership status is evaluated at the moment the return is initiated. A customer who was a Premium Member at the time of purchase but has since cancelled receives only the standard window. Conversely, a customer who joins Premium after purchase but before initiating the return does receive the extended window.

2.3 Free Return Shipping

Premium Members receive free return shipping on all eligible returns, including non-defective returns. Standard accounts pay return shipping or have $7.95 deducted from the refund for a prepaid label. The large-item pickup fee of $29.95 is waived for Premium Members on items under 50 lbs; items over 50 lbs incur a reduced fee of $14.95.

2.4 Pricing and Early Access

  • Member pricing: Exclusive discounts averaging 5–12% on selected categories, displayed as "Premium Price" on product pages.
  • Early access: 24-hour early access to seasonal sales, doorbuster events and limited-release product launches.
  • Extended price match: Price match window extended from 14 days to 30 days after purchase. See the Price Match Policy for competitor eligibility rules.
  • Birthday credit: $10.00 store credit issued annually during the member's birthday month, valid for 60 days.

2.5 Priority Support

Premium Members are routed to a priority support queue with a target first-response time of 2 minutes for live chat and 4 hours for email, compared with 12 minutes and 24 hours for standard accounts. Premium Business members receive a named account representative for orders exceeding $2,000 annually.

2.6 Warranty Benefits

Premium Members receive a 10% discount on all ShopSmart Protect extended warranty plans purchased at checkout. The ShopSmart Protect enrollment window is extended from 30 days to 60 days after the original product purchase for Premium Members.

3. Billing and Renewal

3.1 Automatic Renewal

All memberships renew automatically on the anniversary of the enrollment date using the payment method on file. A renewal reminder email is sent 7 days before the charge. If the payment method fails, ShopSmart will retry three times over 10 days before suspending membership benefits.

3.2 Cancellation

Membership may be cancelled at any time through Account Settings or by contacting customer support. Cancellation rules:

  • Cancelled within 14 days of initial enrollment with no benefits used: full refund of the membership fee.
  • Cancelled within 14 days of enrollment with benefits used (free expedited shipping, member pricing, or an extended-window return): prorated refund minus the retail value of benefits consumed.
  • Cancelled after 14 days: benefits continue until the end of the paid term; no refund is issued.
  • Monthly plans: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month; no partial-month refunds.

3.3 Benefit Lapse on Cancellation

When a membership lapses or is cancelled, extended return windows revert to standard windows immediately. A return initiated after the lapse date is evaluated under the standard window even if the order was placed during the active membership period.

4. Restrictions and Termination

4.1 Fair Use

Membership benefits are intended for personal or single-business use. ShopSmart may suspend or terminate a membership for:

  1. Sharing account credentials across unrelated households or businesses
  2. Reselling products purchased at member pricing in commercial volume without a Premium Business membership
  3. Return rates exceeding 40% of orders over a rolling 12-month period
  4. Repeated chargebacks or payment disputes found to be invalid
  5. Abuse of the price match or price adjustment programs

Memberships terminated for cause are not refunded. Affected accounts may be barred from re-enrolling for 24 months.

4.2 Geographic Limitations

Premium shipping benefits apply to domestic addresses only. International orders placed by Premium Members receive a 15% discount on international shipping rates but are not eligible for free shipping. Extended return windows apply internationally; however, the customer remains responsible for international return shipping and any customs duties.
