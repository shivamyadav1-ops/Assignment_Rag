# ShopSmart Electronics — Price Match & Price Adjustment Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2024
**Policy Version:** 2.4

---

## 1. Price Match Guarantee

ShopSmart Electronics will match the advertised price of an identical in-stock item sold by an eligible competitor. The price match applies at the time of purchase or retroactively as a price adjustment within the applicable window.

### 1.1 Price Match Windows

| Account Type | Price Match Window | Price Adjustment Window |
|---|---|---|
| Standard account | 14 days from delivery | 14 days from delivery |
| Premium Member | 30 days from delivery | 30 days from delivery |
| Premium Business | 30 days from delivery | 30 days from delivery |
| Premium Student | 30 days from delivery | 30 days from delivery |

The window is measured from the **delivery date**, not the order date. For items not yet delivered, the window opens upon delivery. For in-store pickup orders, the window is measured from the pickup date.

### 1.2 What Qualifies as a Match

To qualify, the competitor's item must be:

1. **Identical** — same brand, model number, color, capacity and configuration
2. **In stock** and available for immediate purchase or delivery at the competitor
3. **Sold and shipped by the competitor directly**, not by a third-party marketplace seller
4. **Advertised publicly** at the lower price, verifiable by ShopSmart staff
5. **Priced in the same currency** and available to the same delivery region

The comparison price includes the item price plus any mandatory shipping charges. A competitor item priced at $340.00 with $15.00 mandatory shipping is compared as $355.00 against a ShopSmart price that includes free shipping.

### 2.2 Excluded Sources

Prices from the following sources are **not eligible** for price matching:

1. Online marketplaces and third-party sellers (including marketplace listings on otherwise-eligible retailer sites)
2. Auction sites, classified listings and peer-to-peer sales
3. Membership-only warehouse clubs requiring paid membership to see or obtain the price
4. Liquidation, going-out-of-business, fire-sale or clearance events
5. Open-box, refurbished, used, demo or display-model pricing when matching a new item
6. Prices requiring a coupon code, rebate, trade-in, bundle purchase or financing enrollment
7. Flash sales, doorbusters and limited-quantity promotions lasting under 6 hours
8. Pricing errors, misprints and prices the competitor has publicly disclaimed
9. International pricing or pricing outside the delivery region
10. Employee, student, military or other restricted-eligibility pricing not available to the general public

## 3. Excluded Products

The following categories are excluded from price matching entirely:

- Gift cards and prepaid cards
- Software licenses and digital download codes
- ShopSmart Protect extended warranty plans
- Membership fees and subscription services
- Installation, delivery and White Glove service fees
- Custom-configured or built-to-order systems
- Items sold as part of a bundle where the bundle components are not individually priced
- Products within 48 hours of a manufacturer-announced price change
- Trade-in credits and promotional credit offers

## 4. How to Request a Price Match

### 4.1 Before Purchase

1. Locate the competitor's advertised price (web page, printed ad, or in-store price tag)
2. Contact customer support by phone, chat, or in-store associate
3. Provide the competitor name, item URL or ad, and the advertised price
4. Verification typically completes within 10 minutes for online prices

### 4.2 After Purchase (Price Adjustment)

1. Log into your ShopSmart account and open Order History
2. Select the order and choose "Request Price Adjustment"
3. Enter the competitor details and advertised price
4. Submit — review is completed within 2 business days

If approved, the difference is refunded to the original payment method. Processing follows the same timelines as standard refunds: 5–7 business days for credit card, 3–5 for PayPal, instant for store credit. Customers may elect to receive the adjustment as store credit for instant processing.

### 4.3 ShopSmart Own-Price Drops

If ShopSmart lowers its own price on an item you purchased, you are entitled to a price adjustment for the difference within the same window shown in Section 1.1. Own-price-drop adjustments do not require competitor verification and are processed automatically within 1 business day of request.

## 5. Limits and Conditions

### 5.1 Quantity Limits

Price matches are limited to **3 units per item per customer per 30-day period**. Price adjustments are limited to the quantity originally purchased. ShopSmart reserves the right to decline matches on quantities that suggest commercial resale.

### 5.2 Interaction with Returns

A price-matched or price-adjusted item remains subject to the standard Return & Refund Policy. If the item is subsequently returned, the refund amount is the **adjusted price actually paid**, not the original list price. Restocking fees, where applicable, are calculated on the adjusted price.

### 5.3 Interaction with Promotions

A price match may not be combined with:

- Promotional discount codes applied to the same item
- Storewide percentage-off sale events
- Bundle pricing or multi-item discounts
- Loyalty point redemptions on the same transaction

A price match may be combined with:

- Free shipping offers
- ShopSmart Protect purchases
- Trade-in credit applied to a separate item
- Premium member pricing, where the member price is higher than the matched price

### 5.4 Final Determination

ShopSmart reserves sole discretion in determining whether a competitor price qualifies. Decisions may be appealed once by contacting a support supervisor. ShopSmart may decline or limit price matching for accounts showing patterns of abuse, including repeated submission of ineligible prices or coordinated claims across linked accounts.
