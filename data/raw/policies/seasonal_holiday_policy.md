# ShopSmart Electronics — Seasonal, Holiday & Peak Period Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2024
**Policy Version:** 1.4

## 1. Extended Holiday Return Window

### 1.1 The Holiday Rule

Items purchased between **November 15 and December 31** of any calendar year may be returned until **January 31 of the following year**, or within the item's standard category return window, whichever date is **later**.

The rule never shortens a window. A Computer Accessory purchased on December 28 has a standard 45-day window expiring February 11; because February 11 is later than January 31, the February 11 date applies. A smartphone purchased on November 20 has a standard 15-day window expiring December 5; because January 31 is later, the January 31 date applies.

| Purchase Date | Category | Standard Expiry | Holiday Expiry | Effective Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 20 | Smartphones & Tablets (15 d) | Dec 5 | Jan 31 | **Jan 31** |
| Dec 1 | Audio & Headphones (30 d) | Dec 31 | Jan 31 | **Jan 31** |
| Dec 28 | Computer Accessories (45 d) | Feb 11 | Jan 31 | **Feb 11** |
| Dec 30 | Gaming Consoles (15 d) | Jan 14 | Jan 31 | **Jan 31** |
| Nov 10 | Audio & Headphones (30 d) | Dec 10 | Not applicable | **Dec 10** |

The window is measured from the **purchase date** for the holiday rule, unlike standard windows which are measured from the delivery date. This is deliberate: it protects gift purchasers who buy early and give the item weeks later.

### 1.2 What the Holiday Rule Does Not Change

The extended window affects the deadline only. It does not change:

- Non-returnable categories — software codes and gift cards remain non-returnable
- Restocking fees — the 15% fee still applies to opened televisions over 55 inches, desktops, large appliances and drones
- Condition requirements — original packaging and accessories are still required
- Proof of purchase requirements
- The 48-hour windows for Dead-on-Arrival and shipping-damage claims, which are unchanged

### 1.3 Interaction with Premium Membership

Premium Member extensions stack with the holiday rule. The effective deadline is the latest of: the standard window, the standard window plus 15 days for Premium, or January 31. A Premium Member buying a smartphone on December 30 has a standard window of 15 days plus 15 Premium days, expiring January 29; January 31 is later, so January 31 applies.

## 2. Gift Returns

### 2.1 Who Can Return a Gift

Returns must be initiated by the account that placed the order, because the refund flows back to the original payment method. A gift recipient cannot initiate a return on an order placed by someone else, and support cannot disclose order details to a non-account-holder. See the Account Security, Privacy & Data Handling Policy for the verification requirements.

### 2.2 Gift Receipts

A gift receipt may be requested at checkout or generated afterwards from Order History. A gift receipt shows the item and order ID but conceals the price paid. Presenting a gift receipt in store allows the recipient to exchange the item or receive **store credit** at the current selling price — never a refund to a card, and never cash.

If the item has been marked down since purchase, store credit is issued at the current lower price. Recipients seeking the full original value must ask the purchaser to process a standard return instead.

### 2.3 Gift Exchanges

A recipient holding a gift receipt may exchange the item in store for a different item in the same category, paying or receiving the difference as store credit. Exchanges follow the same deadline as returns, including the holiday extension.

## 3. Peak Period Operations

### 3.1 Processing Delays

During the peak period from **November 15 to December 31**, order processing extends from the usual 1–2 business days to up to **3 business days**. Shipping transit times are unchanged, but carrier networks experience their own delays, and published transit estimates become less reliable during the final two weeks of December.

### 3.2 Delivery Guarantees

Next-Day and Premium Same-Day shipping guarantees are suspended from **December 18 to December 26** due to carrier capacity constraints. Orders placed with these services during that window are charged the standard shipping rate instead, with the difference refunded automatically within 3 business days. Customers are notified at checkout when the guarantee is suspended.

### 3.3 Ordering Deadlines for Holiday Delivery

| Shipping Method | Last Order Date for Dec 24 Delivery |
|---|---|
| Standard Shipping | December 14 |
| Expedited Shipping | December 19 |
| Next-Day Shipping | December 22 |
| Store Pickup (BOPIS) | December 24, by 2:00 PM local |

These dates assume the item is in stock at the time of order. Backordered and pre-order items carry no holiday delivery guarantee.

## 4. Seasonal Sale Events

### 4.1 Event Calendar

| Event | Typical Dates | Premium Early Access | Price Match Eligible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring Clearance | Mid-March, 7 days | 24 hours early | Yes |
| Mid-Year Sale | Early July, 5 days | 24 hours early | Yes |
| Back to School | Mid-August, 14 days | 24 hours early | Yes |
| Black Friday | Late November, 4 days | 24 hours early | Limited — see 4.2 |
| Cyber Monday | Monday after Black Friday | 24 hours early | Limited — see 4.2 |
| Year-End Clearance | December 26 – January 5 | 24 hours early | No |

### 4.2 Price Matching During Sale Events

Doorbuster and flash promotions lasting under 6 hours are not eligible for price matching, and neither are liquidation or clearance events. During Black Friday and Cyber Monday, most competitor promotions fall into one of these excluded categories, so price match requests are frequently declined during that period. ShopSmart's own-price-drop adjustment still applies: if ShopSmart lowers its price on an item a customer already bought, the difference is adjustable within the customer's standard window.

Year-End Clearance pricing is explicitly excluded from both competitor price matching and own-price-drop adjustment, because clearance pricing is below the normal selling price and is offered on an as-is basis.

### 4.3 Sale Items and Returns

Items purchased at a sale price are returnable under the standard category window unless explicitly marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance — No Returns" on the product page at the time of purchase. The refund is the amount actually paid, not the list price. Items marked Final Sale are non-returnable except when defective, in which case the Standard Warranty and Dead-on-Arrival provisions still apply in full.

## 5. Inventory and Availability During Peak

### 5.1 Stock Accuracy

Inventory counts update every 15 minutes during normal operation and every 5 minutes during peak events. Despite this, high-demand items occasionally oversell. When an order cannot be fulfilled, it is cancelled automatically, the authorization hold released, and the customer notified by email. ShopSmart does not substitute a different model without the customer's agreement.

### 5.2 Purchase Limits

Quantity limits are applied to high-demand items during sale events, typically 1–3 units per customer per event. Orders exceeding the limit are cancelled in whole or in part. Limits are enforced across linked accounts sharing a payment method or delivery address.
