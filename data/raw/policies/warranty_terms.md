# ShopSmart Electronics — Warranty Terms & Conditions

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Document Version:** 2.1

## 1. ShopSmart Standard Warranty

Every product sold by ShopSmart Electronics comes with the manufacturer's original warranty. In addition, ShopSmart provides a **90-day ShopSmart Standard Warranty** on all products, covering:

- Defects in materials and workmanship
- Premature hardware failure under normal use
- Factory-defective batteries that fail to hold a charge

The ShopSmart Standard Warranty begins on the date of delivery and runs concurrently with (not in addition to) the manufacturer warranty.

### 1.1 What Is NOT Covered by the Standard Warranty

- Accidental damage (drops, spills, impacts, cracked screens)
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality (scratches, dents)
- Damage caused by unauthorized modifications, repairs, or software alterations
- Consumable parts (batteries beyond factory defects, stylus tips, ear cushions)
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage caused by use outside the product's intended purpose
- Software issues, viruses, or data loss
- Damage caused by power surges or improper voltage

## 3. Filing a Warranty Claim

### 3.1 Standard Warranty Claims

To file a claim under the ShopSmart Standard Warranty:

1. Contact customer support with your order ID and description of the issue
2. Our support team will attempt to troubleshoot the issue remotely
3. If the issue cannot be resolved, a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) will be issued
4. Ship the product using the prepaid return label provided
5. Upon inspection, ShopSmart will either repair or replace the product

**Processing time:** 7–14 business days from receipt of the returned item.

### 3.2 ShopSmart Protect Claims

To file a claim under ShopSmart Protect:

1. Log into your ShopSmart account and navigate to "My Warranties"
2. Select the product and click "File a Claim"
3. Describe the issue and upload photos if applicable
4. A claim representative will contact you within 1 business day
5. Follow the instructions provided (repair, replacement, or credit)

**Processing time:** 5–10 business days. Protect Plus customers with loaner eligibility receive the loaner device within 2 business days.

### 3.3 Claim Resolution Options

Depending on the product and availability, ShopSmart may resolve warranty claims through:

- **Repair:** The original product is repaired and returned to the customer
- **Replacement:** A new or certified refurbished unit of the same model is provided
- **Store Credit:** If the product is discontinued and no equivalent is available, store credit equal to the original purchase price is issued

ShopSmart reserves the right to choose the resolution method. Replacement products carry the remainder of the original warranty period, not a new warranty.

## 5. Warranty Transferability

### 5.1 Standard and Manufacturer Warranties

Manufacturer warranties are generally non-transferable and tied to the original purchaser. If you sell or give away a product, the new owner may not be eligible for warranty service.

### 5.2 ShopSmart Protect

ShopSmart Protect plans are **transferable one time** to a new owner. To transfer:

1. The original owner must contact customer support
2. Provide the new owner's name and email address
3. The transfer is processed within 2 business days
4. The new owner receives confirmation and full access to file claims

No fee is charged for the transfer, but it can only be done once per plan.

## 6. Voiding Warranty

The following actions void all ShopSmart warranties (Standard and Protect):

1. Opening the product case or removing screws (unless user-serviceable as stated in the product manual)
2. Repair or modification by unauthorized service providers
3. Use of non-approved accessories or parts that cause damage
4. Removal or alteration of serial number labels
5. Commercial use of a product sold for personal/consumer use
6. Submitting fraudulent claims or providing false information
