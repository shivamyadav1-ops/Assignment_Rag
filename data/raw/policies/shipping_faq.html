Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! Standard shipping is free on all domestic orders of $35 or more. Premium Members enjoy free expedited shipping (2–3 business days) on every order with no minimum purchase requirement.

Do you ship to P.O. boxes?

Standard shipping can deliver to P.O. boxes. However, expedited, next-day, and same-day shipping require a physical street address because these services use carriers that cannot deliver to P.O. boxes.

Order Processing

How long does it take for my order to be processed?

Most orders are processed within 1–2 business days after placement. During peak periods (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holiday season November–December), processing may take up to 3 business days.

Orders placed after 2:00 PM EST on business days will begin processing the following business day. Orders placed on weekends or federal holidays begin processing the next business day.

Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement, provided they have not yet entered the "Processing" stage. Once an order shows status "Processing" or "Shipped," it cannot be cancelled and must go through the standard return process after delivery.

To request a cancellation, contact customer support immediately or use the "Cancel Order" button in your Order History (available only while the order is in "Confirmed" status).

What does each order status mean?

  • Confirmed: Your order has been received and payment authorized. It can still be cancelled.
  • Processing: Your order is being picked and packed at our warehouse. Cancellation is no longer possible.
  • Shipped: Your order has left our warehouse. A tracking number has been emailed to you.
  • In Transit: Your package is with the carrier and on its way.
  • Out for Delivery: Your package will be delivered today.
  • Delivered: Your package has been delivered to the address on file.
  • Return Initiated: A return has been requested for this order.

Tracking Your Order

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number and a direct link to the carrier's tracking page. You can also track your order by:

  1. Logging into your ShopSmart account
  2. Going to "Order History"
  3. Clicking on the order number
  4. Selecting "Track Package"

My tracking hasn't updated in several days. What should I do?

Tracking information may take 24–48 hours to update after shipment. If your tracking has not updated for more than 3 business days, please contact our customer support team. We will open an investigation with the carrier and, if the package is confirmed lost, either reship the order or issue a full refund at your preference.

What if my package is marked as delivered but I didn't receive it?

If tracking shows "Delivered" but you have not received your package:

  1. Check around your delivery location (porch, mailbox, with neighbors, building reception)
  2. Wait 24 hours — sometimes packages are scanned as delivered prematurely
  3. If still not found after 24 hours, contact us. We will file a carrier claim and either reship the item or issue a refund. Claims must be filed within 7 days of the delivery date shown in tracking.

Delivery Issues

My order arrived damaged. What do I do?

If your order arrives with visible damage to the outer packaging or the product inside:

  1. Photograph the damaged packaging and product before discarding anything
  2. Contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery
  3. We will arrange a free return pickup and send a replacement or issue a full refund

Damaged items are handled under our Dead-on-Arrival (DOA) policy. See our Return & Refund Policy for full details.

I received the wrong item. What should I do?

If you received an item different from what you ordered, contact us immediately. We will:

  • Send a prepaid return label for the incorrect item
  • Ship the correct item at no additional charge with expedited shipping
  • If the correct item is out of stock, issue a full refund

Please do not discard the incorrect item until the return has been processed.

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Delivery address changes are possible only while the order is in "Confirmed" status (typically within 1 hour of placement). Once the order is "Processing" or "Shipped," the address cannot be changed. For shipped orders, you may be able to redirect through the carrier's website using your tracking number.

Who is responsible for customs duties and taxes?

The recipient is responsible for all customs duties, import taxes, and brokerage fees. These charges are determined by your country's customs authority and are not included in ShopSmart's shipping charges. ShopSmart has no control over these charges and cannot predict their amount.

Are all products available for international shipping?

Most products ship internationally. However, the following restrictions apply:

  • Lithium batteries exceeding 100Wh cannot be shipped by air to certain destinations
  • Some products have regional licensing restrictions (certain software, gaming consoles with region locks)
  • Large or heavy items (over 70 lbs or exceeding 108 inches in combined length and girth) may not be eligible for international shipping

Special Delivery Services

Do you offer in-home setup for large items?

Yes, ShopSmart offers White Glove Delivery for select large items, including televisions over 55 inches, home theater systems, and large appliances. White Glove service includes:

  • Scheduled delivery appointment (4-hour window)
  • In-home delivery to the room of your choice
  • Unboxing and basic setup
  • Removal of all packaging materials

White Glove Delivery costs $79.99 and is available in most metro areas. Schedule at checkout or by contacting customer support.

Can I pick up my order at a ShopSmart store?

Yes! Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store (BOPIS) is available at all ShopSmart retail locations. Select "Store Pickup" at checkout. Your order will be ready within 2 hours for in-stock items. You'll receive an email notification when your order is ready. Orders not picked up within 7 days will be cancelled and refunded.
